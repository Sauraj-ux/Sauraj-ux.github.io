About Me

Welcome! I am a radio astronomer working on galaxy evolution using neutral hydrogen (HI), gravitational lensing, and next-generation radio telescopes such as the Square Kilometre Array (SKA), MeerKAT, and uGMRT. My research focuses on detecting HI emission from distant galaxies, studying galaxy evolution across cosmic time, and developing new techniques for future SKA surveys.

Research Interests

  • Neutral Hydrogen (HI) in galaxies
  • Galaxy evolution
  • Strong gravitational lensing
  • Square Kilometre Array (SKA)
  • Cosmic magnetic fields
  • Large-scale simulations and mock catalogues
